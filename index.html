Welcome to Art Museum



Introductory

The Musée d'Orsay, located in the heart of Paris along the Seine River, is one of the world's most renowned and beloved art museums. Housed within a magnificent former railway station built for the 1900 World's Fair, the museum retains its grand industrial architecture, soaring glass ceilings, and iconic monumental clocks. It is celebrated globally for holding the largest collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist masterpieces in existence, featuring legendary works by artists such as Claude Monet, Vincent van Gogh, Paul Cézanne, and Auguste Renoir, alongside remarkable sculptures, decorative arts, and photography from the late 19th and early 20th centuries.
