Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch post-impressionist painter who is among the most famous and influential figures in the history of Western art. His most famous works include "Starry Night," "Sunflowers," and "The Bedroom." Van Gogh's bold colors and expressive brushwork have had a lasting impact on 20th-century art.

Claude Monet

Claude Monet was a French painter and a founder of Impressionism, a movement that revolutionized French painting in the late 19th century. His most famous works include "Impression, Sunrise," "Water Lilies," and "The Japanese Bridge." Monet's innovative use of color and light has had a profound influence on modern art.

Paul Cézanne

Paul Cézanne was a French painter and a key figure in the transition from 19th-century Impressionism to 20th-century Cubism. His most famous works include "The Bathers," "Still Life with Apples," and "Mont Sainte-Victoire." Cézanne's innovative approach to form and color has had a lasting impact on modern art.
